## About Me

I am a **PhD student in Chemical Engineering Sciences at the University of Guanajuato**, Mexico, and a **Chemical Process Engineer** working at the intersection of process systems engineering, process simulation, optimization, and sustainable process design.

I am also a **part-time lecturer at the University of Engineering and Technology (UTEC)** in Peru, where I teach **Chemical Process Modeling and Simulation** and **Chemical Process Analysis**. My teaching activities focus on the application of process simulation, engineering analysis, and computational tools to chemical engineering problems.

Since March 2025, I have worked as a **Process Engineer at STEMROCK**, where my professional activities are focused on **oil and gas, process engineering, process simulation, and technical and academic development**. STEMROCK works in areas including petroleum and energy, natural gas, process modeling, digitalization, machine learning, and engineering solutions.

## Research

My doctoral research focuses on developing rigorous and sustainable methodologies for the **design and optimization of large-scale chemical processes**. My work combines process simulation with economic, environmental, energetic, and efficiency criteria through **4E analysis and GREENSCOPE**.

A major research project developed between 2022 and 2024 focused on the combined production of **acetone, butanol, and ethanol (ABE) and succinic acid (AS) from lignocellulosic biomass**. The methodology integrates rigorous process simulation, kinetic models, systematic identification of high-impact variables, neural-network-based economic estimation, and multiobjective optimization.

My current research also explores the integration of **machine learning, neural networks, uncertainty analysis, and computational optimization** for chemical-process economic evaluation. Recent work includes the use of parallel computing to accelerate simulation-based optimization and the development of predictive models for equipment and investment costs.

## Research Philosophy

I am interested in connecting **fundamental chemical-engineering models, rigorous process simulation, and modern computational methods** to develop practical solutions for complex engineering problems.

My goal is to improve the **rigor, computational efficiency, economic performance, and sustainability** of chemical-process design while integrating data-driven methods such as machine learning with established engineering models.

I am particularly interested in research and engineering problems where **simulation, optimization, artificial intelligence, and process engineering** can be combined to support better technical decisions.
