This research investigated hydrogen cyanide adsorption on the B23N5 fullerene using computational methods and density functional theory (DFT), with emphasis on the effect of homonuclear bonds.

Following the regional AIChE competition, the work advanced to the 2020 AIChE Annual Student Conference, where Giussepi Mamani Paco represented Peru and UTEC.
