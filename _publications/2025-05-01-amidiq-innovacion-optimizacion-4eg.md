This presentation described the application of 4EG indicators within GREENSCOPE to the optimization of a large-scale process for the combined production of acetone, butanol, ethanol (ABE), and succinic acid (AS).

The work was presented in the Process Engineering, Simulation and Control area of the XLVI Encuentro Nacional de la AMIDIQ in Los Cabos, Baja California.
