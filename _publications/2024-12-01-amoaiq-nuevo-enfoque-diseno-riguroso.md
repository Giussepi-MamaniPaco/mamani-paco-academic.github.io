This poster presented a rigorous and sustainable strategy for the design and optimization of large-scale chemical processes, with application to the combined production of acetone, butanol, and ethanol (ABE) and succinic acid from lignocellulosic biomass.

The work integrates rigorous process simulation, kinetic modeling, 4E sustainability indicators, high-impact-variable identification, economic estimation, and multiobjective optimization.

The AMOAIQ technical program identifies the work as poster ESTOC-37. The researcher's CONCYTEC CTI profile records third place in the poster category.
