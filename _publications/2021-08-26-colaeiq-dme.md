This work presented a comparative economic assessment of two direct dimethyl ether (DME) synthesis processes using emissions from cement plants.

The presentation received first place in the presentation competition of the XXVI Congreso Latinoamericano de Estudiantes de Ingeniería Química (COLAEIQ), organized from Paraguay in virtual format.
