This presentation introduced a parallel-computing approach for accelerating process simulation and optimization by integrating MATLAB and Aspen Plus.

The work focused on reducing computational time in simulation-based optimization and was presented in the Process Engineering, Simulation and Control area of the XLVI Encuentro Nacional de la AMIDIQ.
