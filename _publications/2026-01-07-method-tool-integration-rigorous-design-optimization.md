This work presents a methodology for rigorous design and optimization of multiunit chemical processes. The approach combines process simulation, identification of high-impact variables, 4E analysis, and parallel computing to improve the computational efficiency of simulation-based optimization.

The methodology is demonstrated for the sustainable design of a process involving the production of acetone, butanol, and ethanol (ABE) and succinic acid from lignocellulosic biomass.
