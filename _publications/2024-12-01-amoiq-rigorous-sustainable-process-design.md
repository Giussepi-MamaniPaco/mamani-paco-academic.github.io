This poster presented a rigorous strategy for the design and optimization of large-scale chemical processes, focusing on the combined production of acetone, butanol, and ethanol (ABE) and succinic acid from lignocellulosic biomass.

The methodology integrates process simulation, kinetic modeling, 4E sustainability indicators, high-impact-variable identification, economic estimation, and multiobjective optimization.

The work received third place in the poster category according to the researcher's CONCYTEC CTI profile.
