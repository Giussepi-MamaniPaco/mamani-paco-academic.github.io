This presentation reported an in silico computational study of hydrogen cyanide adsorption on the B23N5 fullerene. The work was presented in the student session of the III Coloquio de Simulaciones Computacionales en Ciencias.

The program identifies Giussepi Mamani Paco from Universidad de Ingeniería y Tecnología (UTEC), Department of Chemical Engineering, as the presenter.
