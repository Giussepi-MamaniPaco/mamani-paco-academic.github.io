This work proposes an artificial neural network approach for the early-stage prediction of equipment installation costs in chemical processes. The models use design and operating variables generated through Aspen Plus and Aspen Process Economic Analyzer simulations.

The study addresses the challenge of estimating process investment costs when detailed industrial cost information is not available during the early stages of process design.
