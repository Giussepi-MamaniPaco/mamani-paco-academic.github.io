This research thesis reviews technologies for carbon dioxide capture and utilization and evaluates process routes for producing dimethyl ether from carbon dioxide and natural gas.

The work examines CO2 capture technologies, amine-based absorption, and routes involving pre-reforming, dry reforming, and direct dimethyl ether synthesis, with emphasis on the process conditions required for the different reaction stages.
